Design your own natural stone bracelet or necklace

MORYUNA is a jewellery brand based in Turkey, focused on handmade natural stone bracelets and necklaces. MORYUNA Studio is an online 3D jewellery designer that lets you explore how a piece might look before it is made. Whether you prefer understated everyday jewellery or want to put together a personal gift, you can experiment with gemstone colours, bead sizes and patterns at your own pace.

Designing a piece takes three steps. First, choose a bracelet or necklace model. You can start with a design inspired by a colour palette, a zodiac sign or the four elements. Next, arrange your natural stone beads one by one, changing their sizes and order to explore different combinations. Finally, adjust the bracelet to your wrist measurement or set your preferred necklace length, and view the design from different angles.

Any symbolic meanings associated with gemstones are presented as cultural traditions, not as claims about their effects. The prices displayed in the studio are illustrative.

How it works

  1. Choose a bracelet or necklace model.
  2. Select natural stone beads, their sizes and arrangement.
  3. Adjust the size and review your design in 3D.

Frequently asked questions

What is MORYUNA Studio and how do I design my own bracelet or necklace?

MORYUNA Studio is a browser-based 3D gemstone bracelet and necklace designer. Choose a model, customise the beads individually and explore your design from different angles.

How do I measure my wrist for a bracelet?

Wrap a flexible tape measure around your wrist where you would wear the bracelet, keeping it snug without tightening it, and note the circumference in centimetres. Use this measurement as a starting point in the studio, bearing in mind that bead size and your preferred fit can affect how a bracelet sits.

Which gemstones can I choose for my design?

The studio catalogue contains natural stone beads that you can use in your design. Browse the catalogue to see the current selection and compare stones by colour and appearance.

How does bead size change the look of a bracelet or necklace?

Smaller beads create a finer look, while larger beads make a more pronounced visual statement. You can compare bead diameters in 3D; the number of beads needed for a given length may also vary with their size.

Can I design a zodiac, birthstone or four-element bracelet?

You can start with a ready-made design inspired by the zodiac, the four elements or a colour palette, and personalise it bead by bead. Birthstones can also inspire your choices; associations with zodiac signs or birth months are symbolic traditions.

Are the prices shown in MORYUNA Studio final?

No, the prices shown are illustrative rather than final retail prices. Confirmed selling prices will be published with the relevant product information once orders open.

When can I order the bracelet or necklace I have designed?

The studio is currently available for designing jewellery, but orders are not being accepted yet. No date for opening orders has been announced.

                                    Düzensiz ve iç kapanımlı parçalar ile kolye uçları kaynak GLB’nin biçimi ve ölçüsüyle dizilir.

                                    +Z deliğinin merkezini, GLB’nin kaynak orijinine göre X ve Y yönünde milimetre olarak gir.

                                    Yayın kopyası, GLB’nin ek JSON bilgilerini ve sıfır hizalama kaydını düzenler. Özgün GLB’yi sakla. Özel 3B efektler varsa kopyanın gömülü görünümünü özgün görsel hedefle karşılaştır.

                                    Aynı GLB’nin görünümünü ve yönünü buradaki açılardan veya yerel bir 3B görüntüleyiciden incele. Kullanım hakkı, GLB’nin gömülü dokularını da kapsamalı.
