Gizlilik: bu cihazda ne saklıyoruz?

Tasarım stüdyosu çerez kullanmaz. Tasarımların yalnız bu cihazda, tarayıcının kendi kayıt alanında saklanır. Stüdyonun kendisi bu kayıtları bir sunucuya göndermez; bir şey gönderecekse sen, bir düğmeye dokunarak gönderirsin.

  • Çerez yok: stüdyo hiçbir çerez yazmaz.
  • Tasarımların bu cihazda kalır; sen göndermedikçe cihazından çıkmaz.
  • Adresi açtığında yalnız tasarla.moryuna.com sunucusuyla konuşulur; üçüncü taraf yazı tipi, analiz, reklam ya da betik yok.

Privacy: what do we store on this device?

The design studio does not use cookies. Your designs are stored only on this device, in your browser’s own storage. The studio itself does not send these records to a server. If anything is sent, you send it by tapping a button.

  • No cookies: the studio does not write any cookies.
  • Your designs stay on this device. They do not leave it unless you send them.
  • When you open the site, your browser communicates only with the tasarla.moryuna.com server. There are no third-party fonts, analytics, ads or scripts.

What is stored on this device

Records that store your designs

The design you are working on

What it stores
The design you are working on: which parts are arranged in which order, its size and its name.
Why
So you can pick up where you left off when you refresh the page or close and reopen it.
How long
Until you delete it; no time limit.
When it is written
Every time you change the design.
Is it needed?
Yes: this feature does not work without it.

My designs (your saved designs)

What it stores
The designs and their versions that you save in My designs, plus an automatic backup of the previous design when you open another in its place (up to three backups).
Why
So you can return to your saved designs later.
How long
Until you delete it.
When it is written
When you tap “Save this design”, and to make a backup when replacing a design.
Is it needed?
Yes: this feature does not work without it.

Optional conveniences

Everything works without these, too. They just make things a little easier.

Original copy of the old design format

What it stores
An exact copy of the original version of a design in the old storage format.
Why
So the original design is not lost when switching to the new storage format.
How long
Until you delete it.
When it is written
Only the first time a design in the old format is overwritten.
Is it needed?
No: it just makes things easier.

“I started designing” flag

What it stores
A single flag (1) showing that you have started designing.
Why
So your next visit opens the design you left off with instead of the first screen.
How long
Until you delete it.
When it is written
When you first edit or select a model.
Is it needed?
No: it just makes things easier.

Your zodiac sign and colour preference

What it stores
Your chosen zodiac sign and colour preference; nothing else.
Why
So stones that match your preferences appear first in the stone list and you do not have to choose again each time.
How long
Until you change or delete it.
When it is written
When you save your zodiac sign and colour preference.
Is it needed?
No: it just makes things easier.
